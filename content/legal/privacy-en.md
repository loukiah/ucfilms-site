<p>Data processing on this website is carried out by the website operator. You can find the operator’s contact details in the “Information on the controller” section of this privacy policy.</p>
<h3>How do we collect your data?</h3>
<p>On the one hand, your data is collected when you provide it to us. This may, for example, be data that you enter in a contact form.<br/>

<p>Cookies may originate from us (first-party cookies) or from third-party companies (so-called third-party cookies). Third-party cookies enable the integration of certain services from third-party companies within websites (e.g. cookies for processing payment services).</p>
